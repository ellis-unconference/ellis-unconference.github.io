ELLIS UnConference

A European Gathering on Machine Learning

December 8, 2026

Paris, France

About the event

The ELLIS UnConference is an annual community gathering, bringing together the European machine learning community to foster exchange, collaboration, and scientific discussion. Following several successful editions in Copenhagen, Spain, Germany and France, the event returns this year as a one-day gathering kicking off the NeurIPS satellite in Paris. Join us for a day of community building, scientific exchange, and the opportunity to present a paper recently accepted at a top machine learning venue.
