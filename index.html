About the event

The UnConference is an annual gathering of the European machine learning community, made possible by the European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems (ELLIS). After a successful first edition in Copenhagen last year, the UnConference is coming back this year as a one day event kicking off the NeurIPS satellite in Paris. Join us for one day of community building and science, and use the opportunity to come present a recent work recently accepted at a top machine learning venue!
